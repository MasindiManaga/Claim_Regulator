# ClaimRegulator Regulatory Research

## Scope

ClaimRegulator is initially designed as a prototype compliance monitoring
system for non-life insurance claims in South Africa.

The system is intended to support claims and compliance personnel by
monitoring claims processes, identifying potential compliance exceptions,
maintaining traceability, and supporting human review.

It does not make final claims or regulatory decisions.

## Research Sources

### Policyholder Protection Rules

Primary research for the prototype is based on the Policyholder Protection
Rules applicable to short-term/non-life insurance claims in South Africa.
